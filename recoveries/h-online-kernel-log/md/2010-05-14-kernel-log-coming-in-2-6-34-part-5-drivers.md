# Kernel Log: Coming in 2.6.34 (Part 5) - Drivers

**Thorsten Leemhuis**

**Hundreds of new and improved drivers significantly improve hardware support in the 2.6.34 kernel.**

In his [release email](http://thread.gmane.org/gmane.linux.kernel/982971) for Linux 2.6.34-rc7 last weekend, Linus Torvalds indicated that version 2.6.34 of the Linux kernel will be released soon. The following article describes the advancements in terms of drivers and their infrastructure and concludes our "Coming in 2.6.34" mini series about the most important new features of Linux 2.6.34. The most important changes in the areas of network support, file systems, storage subsystem, graphics hardware and architecture as well as virtualisation were discussed in parts [one](../site/open/features/Kernel-Log-Coming-in-2-6-34-Part-1-Network-Support-975937.html "Kernel Log: Coming in 2.6.34 (Part 1) - Network Support – 13 April 2010"), [two](../site/open/features/Kernel-Log-Coming-in-2-6-34-Part-2-File-Systems-985128.html "Kernel Log: Coming in 2.6.34 (Part 2) - File Systems – 23 April 2010"), [three](../site/open/features/Kernel-Log-Coming-in-2-6-34-Part-3-Graphics-994262.html "Kernel Log: Coming in 2.6.34 (Part 3) - Graphics – 06 May 2010") and [four](../site/open/features/Kernel-Log-Coming-in-2-6-34-Part-4-Architecture-and-virtualisation-998583.html "Kernel Log: Coming in 2.6.34 (Part 4) - Architecture and virtualisation – 12 May 2010") of the series, along with the drivers associated with these kernel areas.

### Audio and video

The [main Git-Pull request](http://thread.gmane.org/gmane.linux.kernel/955742) by audio subsystem maintainer Takashi Iwai lists the most important new features of the kernel's audio drivers, which are now [on the same level](http://git.kernel.org/linus/4757968dbff3d43f373f08de973014a9bd41ef0a) as version 1.0.22.1 of the ALSA drivers.

For instance, [the sound drivers now support](http://git.kernel.org/linus/25045705d4053925a617ed71c5e4b6888e468765) 8-channel audio output via HDMI for GeForce graphics cards with GT21x GPUs as well as for NVIDIA's series MCP89 motherboard chip-sets. The kernel now offers the first components of the [driver code](http://git.kernel.org/linus/53ee98fe8ac77d00bacc1c814d450d83cbd193d4) required to support version 2.0 of the USB audio specification. New too is [support](http://git.kernel.org/linus/d1db38c015a392b0ea8c15ab95abb3ee768b8d47) for the Xonar DS sound card by ASUS and [a driver](http://git.kernel.org/linus/63978ab3e3e963db28093b53bb4598f2702e1ad7) for the Edirol UA-101 audio/MIDI interface.

The V4L/DVB subsystem, which handles TV hardware, was supplemented with the [tlg2300](http://git.kernel.org/linus/5b3f03f044ad6dffc8cd8c9c50bc5d7769cbd89f) drivers for the Telegent Tlg2300 chip as well as the nGene driver for the identically named chip by Micronas ([1](http://git.kernel.org/linus/dae52d009fc950b5c209260d50fcc000f5becd3c), [2](http://git.kernel.org/linus/83f3c7157e087ace8fc67fd759bc3d7b3b64531e)). The developers have also added the [cpia1 Gspca subdriver](http://git.kernel.org/linus/54e8bc5d64a651e2fb8b2366637e6a7d920a4c70) for CpiA webcam chips; this chip was previously handled by cpia, a driver which has now been marked as deprecated; [various other webcam drivers](http://git.kernel.org/linus/6b54ee082543294a04ea1d758abdc6013cbb4e2e) for which the kernel offers different Gspca-based drivers have also been tagged this way.

### Input devices and notebooks

The input subsystem [has been given drivers](http://thread.gmane.org/gmane.linux.kernel/954266) for touch screen interfaces by various vendors. Also new are drivers for Apple's [Magic Mouse](http://git.kernel.org/linus/128537cea464d919febeaea2000e256749f317eb) and [Logitech's G940 Flight System](http://git.kernel.org/linus/74f292ca8c7a2b9370f80d97a49e48174f4c7635).

Vendor-specific notebook drivers will now be maintained [by Matthew Garrett](http://thread.gmane.org/gmane.linux.kernel/954827) as part of the "[x86 platform drivers](http://git.kernel.org/linus/d094485323a1f2abc7e4665700d6036de36fdaef)" subsystem. This subsystem now includes a simple [eeepc-wmi driver](http://git.kernel.org/linus/ee027e4aed3077c4bb3b585c67528eec0e7222df) which previously only controlled display brightness and works with recent ASUS Eee PCs that use WMI for the function keys. Numerous detail improvements were made to the drivers for notebooks by ASUS, Dell, Lenovo/IBM, MSI and Toshiba; these drivers were previously handled by the ACPI maintainer.

### USB and staging

The xHCI driver for USB 3.0 controller chips, which was previously called xhci, has been renamed xhci_hcd to match the naming scheme used for other USB host controller drivers. A [modification implemented](http://git.kernel.org/linus/a4a47bc03fe520e95e0c4212bf97c86545fb14f9) by Torvalds himself now causes the USB storage driver to wait just one second instead of the previous five before it addresses a newly connected USB memory device. Various distributions reduced or completely disabled the delay quite some time ago. Torvalds says it is unclear whether devices that require such a delay are still in use; it could be that this issue is purely historical. The modification is designed to help clarify this question; "*Let's see if anybody screams*" said Torvalds.

[The crystalhd driver](http://git.kernel.org/linus/7963eb432be2ebbf7e740bcdf6b385cc68fb7627) for the Broadcom Crystal HD video accelerator has been added to the staging branch. Major improvements – [including one](http://git.kernel.org/linus/530f43a8a69d9fa10ec6d30652244bb5e2a29694) which enhances performance – were made to the udlfb DisplayLink driver, which is also located in the staging area ([1](http://git.kernel.org/linus/59277b679f8b5ce594e367759256668eba652d0d), [2](http://git.kernel.org/linus/7d9485e2c53caaaed9606a39de1c2746cc9ba262), [3](http://git.kernel.org/linus/3e8f3d6fa672896096068296658a769649186150)). [Various changes](http://git.kernel.org/linus/65a4378471510787e080188f933913315f1afa0c) to the rtl8192e driver are to considerably reduce the power consumption of the identically named Realtek chips during operation. The kernel developers have removed the [altpciechdma](http://git.kernel.org/linus/ca1f29c01aaa37d522dc6ce311769731f5f1919e), [b3dfg](http://git.kernel.org/linus/a945db65436a2666927b555d752e67a1bd193a9c) [mimio](http://git.kernel.org/linus/e37bcc0de040127281de13a84a608570355c20eb) and [p9auth](http://git.kernel.org/linus/2d629030ca649bd4a7356befedbe7bbefa840b21) staging drivers because nobody showed any interest in developing them further. A new addition is the [dt3155 driver](http://git.kernel.org/linus/aa337ef1fbc9650f8b5b9087af1dac33f71eb01c) for DT3155 Digitisers.

### In brief

- Drivers and extensions for Intel's motherboard chip-sets so far only referred to as Cougar Point and expected to be released early next year have been added to various subsystems.
- The AHCI driver [now offers](http://git.kernel.org/linus/d6ef31539d715db7a0eb2d67a1a008c9d8ccf059) FIS-based switching – a technology for addressing multiple hard disks via one SATA connection which is used by some external disk housings with SATA port multipliers.
- Performance improvements to the CFQ I/O scheduler are among the major changes to the block layer, highlighted by maintainer Jens Axboe in [his main Git-Pull request](http://thread.gmane.org/gmane.linux.kernel/955746).
